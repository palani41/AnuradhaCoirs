Who We Are

Welcome to Anuradha Coirs

ISO Certified 50+ Countries Export Quality

Anuradha Coirs is a trusted manufacturer and exporter of premium quality coconut-based products from India. With a strong commitment to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction, we supply high-grade coir and coconut products to buyers across various international markets.

Located in the heart of South India’s coconut-growing region, Anuradha Coirs combines traditional expertise with modern processing methods to deliver eco-friendly and export-quality products suitable for agriculture, horticulture, hydroponics, industrial applications, and commercial trading.

Our wide range of products includes Coco Peat Blocks, Coco Peat Briquettes, Coir Fibre, Grow Bags, Open Top Grow Bags, Coco Husk Chips, Coco Coins & Discs, Semi Husked Coconuts, Fully Husked Coconuts, Tender Coconuts, and Coconut Saplings. Every product is carefully processed, tested, and packed to meet international quality standards and buyer requirements.

Our Manufacturing Process

At Anuradha Coirs, we follow a systematic 10-step manufacturing process — from coconut husk collection and fibre extraction to compression, quality inspection, packing, and container loading — ensuring premium products for international markets.

Every stage is carefully monitored for consistent quality, proper moisture control, and export-standard processing. We offer phytosanitary documentation, fumigation support, and flexible shipment through major Indian ports.
