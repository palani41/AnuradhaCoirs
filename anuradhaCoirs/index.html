Who We Are

Welcome to Anuradha Coirs

ISO Certified 50+ Countries Export Quality

Anuradha Coirs is a trusted manufacturer and exporter of premium quality coconut-based products from India. With a strong commitment to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction, we supply high-grade coir and coconut products to buyers across various international markets.

Located in the heart of South India’s coconut-growing region, Anuradha Coirs combines traditional expertise with modern processing methods to deliver eco-friendly and export-quality products suitable for agriculture, horticulture, hydroponics, industrial applications, and commercial trading.

Our wide range of products includes Coco Peat Blocks, Coco Peat Briquettes, Coir Fibre, Grow Bags, Open Top Grow Bags, Coco Husk Chips, Coco Coins & Discs, Semi Husked Coconuts, Fully Husked Coconuts, Tender Coconuts, and Coconut Saplings. Every product is carefully processed, tested, and packed to meet international quality standards and buyer requirements.

Premium Export Quality

Every product batch passes rigorous laboratory quality checkups. We test for electrical conductivity (EC), moisture content, buffering standards, and expansion capacity to guarantee that every shipment meets premium international agricultural standards.

Competitive Pricing

Operating as a direct manufacturer with our own drying fields, processing machinery, and hydraulic balers allows us to bypass intermediaries. This direct supply chain passes structural cost savings straight to importers and nurseries worldwide.

Custom Packaging & OEM

We customize grow bag specifications, compressed block ratios, briquette designs, and polybag prints. Our private labeling support enables distributors and brands to configure retail-ready sizes according to target markets.

On-Time Delivery

With structured warehousing and proximity to major Southern Indian deep-water ports (Tuticorin and Cochin), we organize freight forwarding, secure vessel space, and complete container loading schedules with high reliability.

Eco-Friendly Products

Our coir fiber and peat substrates are 100% natural, biodegradable organic crop boosters. By reclaiming raw coconut husks, we support closed-loop farming and assist the global agricultural market in transitioning to carbon-neutral soil mediums.

Global Export Support

We navigate phytosanitary certification requirements, professional fumigation parameters, certificates of origin, and custom declarations. Our experience guarantees seamless transit across customs hubs in Europe, the Americas, and Asia.

Flexible Specifications

We blend coco peat, coco chips, and coco fiber ratios in custom percentages depending on your botanical crops. We deliver washed, unwashed, or calcium-buffered substrates to match specific plant rooting parameters.

Long-Term Partnerships

We believe in building sustainable business relationships. We support verified distributors with flexible credit policies, supply chain continuity guarantees, and direct technical consultations for wholesale growers.
