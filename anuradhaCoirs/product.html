Product 01

Coconut Fibre

Extracted from the fibrous husk of the coconut, our coir fibre is processed, graded, and prepared for a wide range of industrial and horticultural uses worldwide.

01 Coconut Fibre

Strong, Sustainable & Versatile

Coconut fibre (coir) is extracted from the outer husk of mature coconuts. Our fibres are carefully processed through defibring, cleaning, grading, and drying to meet international export standards. Available in both Brown Coir (from mature husks) and White Coir (from green husks), each type serves distinct markets.

With exceptional tensile strength, natural resistance to saltwater, and outstanding durability, coir fibre is a preferred raw material for rope-making, geotextiles, floor coverings, brushes, and growing media across the globe.

Product 02

Coco Peat Coin

Compact, lightweight, and incredibly powerful — our coco peat coins expand up to 7× their original size when hydrated, making them the ideal growing medium for modern growers.

02 Coco Peat Coin

Just Add Water — Watch it Grow

Our coco peat coins are compressed discs manufactured from washed, low-EC coco peat. Lightweight and easy to store, each coin expands 5–7× its original volume when water is added, providing a ready-to-use, sterile growing medium in seconds.

Widely used in home gardening kits, seed starting trays, nurseries, and hydroponic setups, our coins are pH-balanced, have excellent air porosity, and retain moisture efficiently — giving seeds the perfect environment to germinate and thrive.

How to Use

Place coin in tray

Add warm water

Wait 5 minutes

Plant your seeds

Product 03

Coir Grow Bag

The professional grower's choice — our coir grow bags deliver superior drainage, root aeration, and reusability for high-value crop production in greenhouses and open fields worldwide.

03 Coir Grow Bag

The Professional Grower's Choice

Our coir grow bags are manufactured from high-quality buffered coco peat, blended with the ideal ratio of coir chips and fibre to achieve optimum air-to-water balance. Delivered pre-moistened and wrapped in UV-treated polyethylene film, they are ready to plant right out of the box.

Compatible with drip-irrigation systems, our grow bags are used extensively for cultivating tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers, strawberries, herbs, and other high-value crops. Each bag can be reused for 2–3 growing cycles, significantly reducing input costs.
