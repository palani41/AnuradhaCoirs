Step by Step

Our 6-Stage Process

 Stage 1

Raw Material

Coconut fibre is highly valued for its capacity to hold moisture and withstand degradation. Known for its robustness and environmentally beneficial qualities, it is perfect for uses including mattress stuffing and soil erosion management. We source only premium-grade coconut husks from trusted farms across Tamilnadu.

 Stage 2

Drying

The fibres are sun-dried or mechanically dried to remove excess moisture, preserving their natural qualities and preparing them for further processing into products like mats, brushes, and geotextiles. Controlled drying ensures optimal moisture content for every product category.

 Stage 3

Washing

The washing process involves thorough rinsing to remove impurities such as dust, sand, and residual husk particles, ensuring cleanliness and improving suitability for manufacturing. Our multi-stage wash cycles produce consistently clean, industry-grade fibre.

 Stage 4

Screening

The screening process separates fibres into different grades based on length and thickness, ensuring uniformity and quality in the final product. Precise grading allows us to match the exact specifications required by each international buyer.

 Stage 5

Quality Control

Rigorous testing and inspection ensure adherence to specifications for moisture content, fibre length, and cleanliness. Every batch is tested before dispatch — only products meeting our strict international standards leave the facility.

 Stage 6

Packing & Stuffing

Proper packing ensures coconut fibre retains its quality and remains intact during shipping, facilitating use in agriculture, horticulture, and manufacturing. Export-compliant packaging protects every product through its global journey.
