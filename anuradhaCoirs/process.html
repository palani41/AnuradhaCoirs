Step by Step

Our 10-Stage Manufacturing Process

01
1Stage One

Coconut Husk Collection

Fresh coconut husks are collected from selected coconut farms and processing centers across South India. Only quality raw materials are chosen for further processing.

Selection of matured coconuts
Removal of damaged husks
Eco-friendly sourcing methods
02
2Stage Two

Husk Separation & Cleaning

The collected coconut husks are separated and cleaned to remove dust, stones, and unwanted impurities before fibre extraction begins.

Raw material inspection
Initial cleaning process
Quality grading of husks
03
3Stage Three

Fibre Extraction

The coconut husks are processed through defibering machines to separate coir fibre from coco peat (coir pith). This is one of the most important stages in coir production.

Mechanical fibre extraction
Separation of fibre and pith
Controlled processing methods
04
4Stage Four

Drying Process

The extracted coir fibre and coco peat are naturally dried under sunlight or processed using controlled drying systems to reduce moisture content to optimal levels.

Moisture reduction to below 15%
Natural sun drying
Quality preservation
05
5Stage Five

Sieving & Screening

Coco peat materials are sieved and screened to remove fine dust, larger particles, and impurities to achieve uniform texture and export quality standards.

Removal of unwanted particles
Uniform particle sizing
Enhanced product quality
06
6Stage Six

Washing & Buffering

Depending on customer requirements, coco peat is washed and buffered to reduce EC levels and improve suitability for horticulture and hydroponic applications.

Low EC processing
Calcium buffering
Suitable for premium agriculture use
07
7Stage Seven

Compression & Block Making

Processed coco peat and coir fibre are compressed using hydraulic machines into blocks, briquettes, grow bags, or bales for easy handling and export transportation.

Hydraulic compression technology
Standardized block sizes
Space-saving packaging solutions
08
8Stage Eight

Quality Inspection

Each product batch undergoes strict quality inspection to verify moisture, EC level, pH value, weight, and overall product consistency before dispatch.

Quality testing procedures
Export standard verification
Product performance checks
09
9Stage Nine

Packing & Labeling

Products are packed using strong export-quality packaging materials with customized labels and buyer-specific specifications to ensure safe transport worldwide.

Export-grade packing
Customized private labeling
Safe and secure packaging
10
10Stage Ten

Container Loading & Export

Finished products are loaded into containers under proper supervision to ensure safe shipment to international destinations across 50+ countries worldwide.

Efficient container loading
Documentation support
Timely export shipment
